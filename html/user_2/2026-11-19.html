With dataset-specific adaptation, JDPI exceeds the released Llama Guard 3-8B baseline by 0.173-0.310 in ROC-AUC across the four benchmarks. In multi-turn replay on TomGibbs, JDPI uses accumulated user history as the conversation unfolds, achieving higher conversation-level accuracy than when it uses the current turn alone. Across 12 directed transfers, JDPI outperforms the classifier by an average of 1.59 percentage points in accuracy, demonstrating stronger cross-dataset robustness.
